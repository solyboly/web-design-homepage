Journal 1

What is a website? From the readings, websites are a holder for information. But I really liked the idea of framing websites as different objects. Many of the things that are used to describe a website, like a plant, or a room, or a shelf, reminds me of an organizing system. And it reminds me of the main focus of my Core III class, which is systems.

Journal 2

"i built this sh*t, brick by brick" the reading sort of made me think of this memed comment, which is usually used when referencing your "for you page" on tiktok and how the algorithm chooses videos based on your interactions with previous videos. I like to think that building something also includes building from your past preferences and experiences and curiosities. Especially in an age where AI can instantly create stuff, building your sh*t brick by brick is that small form of resistence.

Journal 3

I like that this reading goes in depth to comparing what the world wide web was to what the world wide web is now. I am a part of the generation that grew up with the internet and social media, so it is interesting to read and conceptualize about another world.I do agree that the user now is just seen as another data point. I feel this personally with social media algorithms almost every day. I pretty much already know at this point that my personal information is probably floating around somewhere.

Journal 4

"Platforms like the WELL, and the World Wide Web to follow, pioneered a community-driven, decentralized media landscape in opposition to the unidirectional television, radio, and printed news outlets. But corporate giants soon realized the power of this new tool for communication. Once again, the grid caught up with those attempting to escape it, and what could have been a peer-maintained, communal space was inverted and twisted for profit."
this stuck out to me as someone who grew up with the version of the web that couldn't escape the corporatization of the internet.
